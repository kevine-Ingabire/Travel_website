Radisson Blu Hotel & Convention Center - Kigali

✨ 1. Unmatched Luxury in the Heart of Kigali

Radisson Blu Hotel is one of Rwanda’s most prestigious 5-star hotels. It blends modern elegance, African warmth, and world-class hospitality, right in the heart of Kigali, one of Africa's cleanest and safest cities.

🌍 2. Ideal Location

  • Located just 5 km from Kigali International Airport – only a 10-minute drive.
  • Connected to the Kigali Convention Centre – perfect for conferences or special events.
  • Nearby attractions: Kigali Genocide Memorial, Inema Arts Center, Kimironko Market.

🛏️ 3. Elegant Rooms & Comfort

  • 292 modern rooms and suites with stunning city views.
  • Features: Wi-Fi, flat-screen TVs, work desks, premium bedding, private balconies.

🍽️ 4. Exquisite Dining Experiences

  • The Larder – international and Rwandan cuisine.
  • Filini – fine Italian dining.
  • Lobby Lounge Bar – coffee, cocktails, quick bites.
  • Pool Bar – snacks and drinks by the water.

💆 5. Relaxation & Wellness

  • Outdoor pool, sun deck, and poolside service.
  • Fitness center and spa for massages and treatments.
  • Garden space to unwind.

👔 6. Perfect for Events & Business

  • Access to Kigali Convention Centre.
  • Over 18 meeting rooms and a grand ballroom.
  • Great for weddings, summits, and business conferences.

✅ 7. Safety & Sustainability

  • “Safe Travels” protocols.
  • Environmentally friendly and community-focused.
  • Multilingual staff: English, French, Kinyarwanda.

⭐ 8. Celebrity & International Recognition

  • Hosted President Paul Kagame, President Macron, and other global figures.
  • Venue for major events like CHOGM and AU summits.
