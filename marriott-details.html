Why Visit Kigali Marriott Hotel, Rwanda

Experience Luxury, Comfort, and Rwandan Hospitality in the Heart of Kigali

🌟 1. 5-Star Luxury in Kigali’s Prime Location

Kigali Marriott Hotel is one of Rwanda’s most prestigious hotels, offering a perfect blend of modern elegance, comfort, and world-class service. It’s located in downtown Kigali, within walking distance of key attractions, government offices, and cultural spots.

🛏️ 2. Spacious & Stylish Rooms

Over 250 luxurious rooms and suites with modern décor, plush bedding, and city or garden views.

  • Flat-screen TVs
  • High-speed Wi-Fi
  • Workspaces
  • Private balconies (in select rooms)

Perfect for both business travelers and tourists.

🍽️ 3. Dining Excellence

  • Soko Restaurant – International cuisine with locally sourced ingredients.
  • Iriba Bar & Terrace – Cocktails, snacks, and relaxing views.
  • Cucina Restaurant – One of Kigali’s best Italian fine-dining experiences.

💆 4. Relaxation & Wellness

  • Outdoor swimming pool with lounge and bar
  • On-site spa with massages and wellness treatments
  • 24/7 fully equipped fitness center

🏛️ 5. Ideal for Events, Conferences, & Weddings

Over 1,200 sqm of event space including ballrooms and meeting rooms. Professional event planners and catering teams. Frequently used for international conferences, business summits, and high-end social events.

🌍 6. Easy Access to Kigali Attractions

  • Kigali Genocide Memorial
  • Kigali City Tower
  • Kandt House Museum
  • Inema Arts Center
  • Convention Centre & government institutions

Just a 15-minute drive from Kigali International Airport.

🧼 7. Clean, Safe & Sustainable

High standards of hygiene and safety under Marriott’s "Commitment to Clean" protocols. Actively supports local culture and sustainability initiatives.

💖 8. Warm Rwandan Hospitality

Friendly, multilingual staff offering personalized service and attention to detail. A place where global luxury meets local charm.
